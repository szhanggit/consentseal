SELAH HEAD SPA provides treatments to clients, many of whom pay through their extended health benefits with [Insurer name]. We are replacing our paper client forms with an electronic process. Before we start using it, we would like to confirm that the records it produces meet your requirements for claims and audits.

### What we are introducing

For each client we keep two forms: a **Health History (intake) form** and a **Consent to Treatment form**.

1. **The client fills in the health history form** on their phone before their visit.
2. **Our staff prepare the consent details** when the appointment is booked: the practitioner and the treatment areas.
3. **The client signs both forms by hand**, with a finger or stylus, in one of two ways:
   - **In the clinic, on our tablet.** A named staff member witnesses the signature. They check the client's photo identification in person, record the type of ID (not the number), and confirm with their personal PIN.
   - **Remotely, on the client's own phone**, once everything is complete. The client is identified by their phone number and email address on file. We record the date, time and device. At the client's first visit afterwards, a staff member checks their photo identification in person and records that check.

   In both cases, before signing, the client:
   - reviews their completed health history form;
   - reads the full consent text, which must be scrolled to the end;
   - confirms each acknowledgement and understanding individually;
   - confirms the prepared treatment areas.

   The signature step is shown only when all information is complete.
4. **Before treatment, the practitioner records that they discussed the treatment with the client**: the condition, the nature of the treatment, its risks and benefits, and the alternatives. They confirm with their personal PIN.
5. **At the moment of signing, the system creates a PDF of our original form, filled in and signed.** It looks exactly like the paper form, with the handwritten signature and date. The PDF is then sealed:
   - A **digital fingerprint** (SHA-256) of the file is recorded. Any later change to the file, however small, would no longer match it.
   - An **independent trusted timestamp** (RFC 3161) from a recognised timestamping authority proves when the document existed.
   - The file is stored in **write-once storage in Canada**, which nobody can alter or delete during the retention period, including us.
   - A **tamper-evident log** records each step: viewed, confirmed, signed (in clinic or remotely), witnessed or later ID-checked, and the practitioner's discussion record.
6. **On request, we can provide any client's signed forms within minutes**, together with the information needed to verify them independently using standard tools.

If a client's health information, the planned treatment or the consent wording changes, the client signs a new version. Earlier versions are kept and remain available.
