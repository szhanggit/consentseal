SELAH HEAD SPA provides treatments to clients, many of whom pay through their extended health benefits with [Insurer name]. We are replacing our paper client forms with an electronic process. Before we start using it, we would like to confirm that the records it produces meet your requirements for claims and audits.

### What we are introducing

For each client we keep two forms: a **Health History (intake) form** and a **Consent to Treatment form**. Under the new process:

1. **The client fills in the health history form** on their phone before their first visit, or at the clinic.
2. **At the clinic, the client signs in person**, by hand, with a finger or stylus on a tablet. Before signing, the client:
   - reviews their completed health history form;
   - reads the full consent text, which must be scrolled to the end;
   - confirms each acknowledgement and understanding individually;
   - selects the treatment areas.
3. **A named staff member witnesses the signature.** They check the client's identification in person, record the type of ID (not the ID number), and confirm with their personal PIN.
4. **At the moment of signing, the system creates a PDF of our original form, filled in and signed.** It looks exactly like the paper form, with the handwritten signature and date. The PDF is then sealed:
   - A **digital fingerprint** (SHA-256) of the file is recorded. Any later change to the file, however small, would no longer match it.
   - An **independent trusted timestamp** (RFC 3161) from a recognised timestamping authority proves when the document existed.
   - The file is stored in **write-once storage in Canada**, which nobody can alter or delete during the retention period, including us.
   - A **tamper-evident log** records when the consent was viewed, confirmed, signed and witnessed.
5. **On request, we can provide any client's signed forms within minutes**, together with the information needed to verify them independently using standard tools.

If a client's health information or the consent wording changes, the client signs a new version. Earlier versions are kept and remain available.
